A Light Little Glossary

No spoilers — just the keys to understanding this world.

Grain
The unique flow every fabric carries. Those who can read the grain see a garment's wounds and memories — the gift Yiseo awakens to in this world.
Mender
One who mends not just a garment's body, but its heart. The oldest craft in Textopia — and one the world had nearly forgotten.
Iron Stitch
A faction that believes only flawless clothing has worth, branding anything outside their standard as 'defective.'
Needle Tower
The tower rising at the center of Cotton City. Every thread on the continent is said to lead here.
Wheel of Fate
A great wheel that turns deep within Knit Mountain, spinning the thread of the world.
Ragged Grave
The last land discarded clothes reach. Yet perhaps not an ending — perhaps a beginning.
