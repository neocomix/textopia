Textopia

A tiny alterations shop in a Seoul alley. On the night a redevelopment notice lands in her hands, the mender Yiseo follows a single thread and tumbles into Textopia, a world where clothes breathe and live.

A torn dress speaks of its wounds; a worn jacket aches for the one who left it. As the shadow of Iron Stitch — which demands nothing but "perfect" clothes — spreads across the land, Yiseo takes up a single needle and begins to mend cloth and heart alike. A gentle adventure that gives discarded things a second name.

Two of us started a publishing house. Three, if you count the AI.

Matthew and Anna were colleagues at the same company. After going independent, they teamed up again — they simply work well together — to build this project side by side. One lays down the story's bones and its systems; the other breathes it to life with the feel of fabric and with pictures. When our hands fall short, AI lends a few more. So we are small, but never slow.

A 'seam allowance' is the little extra you leave behind to finish a garment. We believe that even discarded cloth — even discarded stories — carry a seam allowance of their own. That is how our publishing house found its name.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this book for?
For anyone who wants to slip away into a gentle world at the end of a tiring day. No cruelty here — only a story of healing and connection.
Will there be a print edition?
We're releasing the ebook first, and depending on the response, we plan to prepare a print edition through a Tumblbug crowdfunding campaign.
How do I read the EPUB from your own store?
Just open the EPUB file you receive after purchase in any EPUB reader — the RIDIBOOKS app, Apple Books, Google Play Books, and more. Each file carries a quiet watermark with the buyer's details.
Are there plans for overseas editions?
Japanese and English translations are in the works. We'll share the news through our newsletter.
